Frequently asked questions

Good to know before booking.

What is a CARB Clean Truck Check?

California’s Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance program requires covered vehicles to meet applicable testing and compliance requirements. Vehicle eligibility and testing obligations depend on the current program rules. We’ll confirm the service needed before scheduling.

Can you test every heavy-duty truck?

Not necessarily. Vehicle year, engine, emissions system, fuel type, and applicable program requirements affect the correct testing method. Share your vehicle details so we can confirm whether we can service your units.

Do you offer fleet pricing?

Yes, fleet quotes may be available when multiple eligible vehicles can be scheduled at one location. Pricing depends on vehicle type, quantity, location, and appointment requirements.

Are you an official CARB program or government agency?

No. FleetCheck Mobile is an independent service provider, not CARB or a government agency. Service availability and any required credentials or equipment must be confirmed before appointments are accepted.
